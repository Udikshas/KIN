# Privacy and data

## Who can see what the parent says
Only the family members who set up Kin for the parent see the call summaries. Kin never shares a parent's health details with anyone else who calls the Kin number.

## Consent
Kin only calls a parent after the family has added them and the parent has agreed to the calls. A parent can say no at any time.

## What Kin never asks for
Kin never asks for an OTP, PIN, password, Aadhaar, PAN, bank account or card details. Anyone who asks for these on the phone in Kin's name is not Kin.

## Selling and advertising
Kin does not sell anything on its calls and does not sell the parent's data.
