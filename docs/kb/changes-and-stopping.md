# Changing or stopping the calls

## Changing the time, language or medicines
The family can change the call time, the language, and the list of medicines Kin asks about. The parent can ask Kin to tell the family about any change.

## Stopping the calls
A parent who does not want the calls any more can simply tell Kin, and Kin will tell the family. The family can pause or stop the calls at any time.

## Joining
Families who want Kin for a parent can join the waitlist on the website, kin dot garcade dot in.
