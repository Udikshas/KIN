# What is Kin

Kin is a gentle daily phone call for an older parent who lives alone. A family member, usually a son or daughter who lives in another city or abroad, sets Kin up for their parent.

## Who Kin is
Kin is an AI helper, not a person and not a doctor. Kin speaks the parent's own language, such as Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, Bengali or English, and calls from the number +91 79714 13869.

## What happens on a call
Kin asks a few friendly questions: how the parent slept, any pain, whether they ate, how they feel, and whether today's medicines were taken. Kin remembers earlier calls, so it can ask how the knee pain from yesterday is now. A call takes about one to five minutes.

## What the family gets
After each call the family gets one short card about what changed: sleep, pain, mood and medicines, with a green, amber or red marker.

## Who made Kin
Kin was built by team Rocket for the Singapore India Hackathon 2026. The website is kin dot garcade dot in.
