# Family updates and alerts

## The evening card
After each call the family gets one short update: how the parent slept, any pain, their mood, whether medicines were taken, and anything the parent asked to pass on.

## Asking for a call from the family
If the parent says they would like their son or daughter to call, Kin passes that wish on. Kin cannot promise when the family will call.

## Urgent situations
If the parent mentions a fall, chest pain, trouble breathing or feeling confused, Kin tells them to call 108 in India straight away and alerts the family. If the family set an emergency contact, Kin can connect the call to them.

## Kin is not an emergency service
In an emergency, call 108 in India or 995 in Singapore.
